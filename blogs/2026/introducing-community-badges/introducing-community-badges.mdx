Hey everyone! It’s [Jackie](https://www.codedex.io/@jackieisonline), Codédex’s Sr. Product Designer, here with an exciting announcement... 

**We just launched 15 new badges!** 🎉

Adding more badges has been a long overdue feature on the docket (what’s a gamified platform without plenty of badges??!).

Before, you could earn badges for completed courses and chapters, or special events like attending a hackathon.

Now, we’ve added some new badges to celebrate involvement in the community and encourage getting connected with other learners. Check them out below, and keep reading for more badge-related updates!

## How to earn community badges

<ImageZoom src="https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/codedex-io.appspot.com/o/blogs%2Fintroducing-community-badges%2Fcommunity%20checklist.png?alt=media&token=c3882bef-7ed5-4412-8f55-f7a97526627d" />

To earn these new badges, complete the actions in the table above! You’ll receive a notification about receiving your new badge. And to know which ones you have yet to earn, you can go to the [Codédex Community](https://www.codedex.io/community) to track your progress towards your next badge.

<ImageZoom src="https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/codedex-io.appspot.com/o/blogs%2Fintroducing-community-badges%2Fcommunity-badge-progress.png?alt=media&token=e9354d00-bc87-4bda-bfcb-7065f1be465a" />

Already an seasoned community member? We’ve counted any existing progress you’ve already made (e.g. likes, comments, followers) and made sure to award any badges you should have earned!

> What’s a gamified platform without plenty of badges??!

## View all your badges

<ImageZoom src="https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/codedex-io.appspot.com/o/blogs%2Fintroducing-community-badges%2Fopen-badges-from-dashboard.gif?alt=media&token=271147d2-4ad4-48f2-84db-7cd801239c5d" />

We’ve also made it better to view and track all of your badges across Codédex. You can now view all your earned badges from the Badges icon on your dashboard, or via your user profile. These include any community badges from the list above, as well as any badges earned from courses or special events.
