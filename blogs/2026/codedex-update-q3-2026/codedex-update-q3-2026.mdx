## 🚀 Builds Editor v1.1

We launched [Codédex Builds v1.0](https://codedex.io/builds) back in June, and we've been making it even better, thanks to your feedback! And get this: **183,000+ Builds** have already been created by the community! 🤯

This quarter, we shipped Builds v1.1 with two exciting additions:

- 🧚 Starter Code Generation with AI
- ⚙️ Community Integration

### 🧚 Starter Code Generation with AI

![Builds landing page](https://i.imgur.com/7xsEtBU.png)

Ever have an idea for a website but don't know where to start? 

With Builds, you can now generate starter HTML, CSS, and JavaScript code in less than 30 seconds. Just describe what you want to create, and let AI help you get the ball rolling.

There are now three ways to get started:

1. **Describe your idea:** Tell Lumi what you want to build. The more details, the better!
2. **Choose a 3 template:** There's a Personal Portfolio, Linktree Clone, and Habit Tracker.
3. **Build from scratch:** Start with a blank canvas and make something entirely your own.

You still need to know all three languages to make the site truly YOURS, but it’s never been easier to turn an idea into a website.

### ⚙️ Builds x Community Integration

![Builds x Community feature](https://i.imgur.com/xPJfmvO.gif)

You can now choose to submit a Build in Monthly Challenge AND Project Showcase.

### 🖼️ Preview Page

So what happens when you submit a Build? 

HTML/CSS/JS Builds now have a Preview page with a thumbnail image and:

- View in Builds
- Live site
- Remix 
- Expanded embed view

Hope to see more awesome Builds coming out of the community in 2026 and beyond!

### New Community Feature: @ Mention

![Mention feature](https://i.imgur.com/4TfaIME.png)

Since we’re on the topic of Community, we’ve added a new feature: @ Mentions!

You can now type **@username** to find and tag someone directly in a community post or comment. They’ll receive a notification letting them know they’ve been tagged.

This makes it easier to bring others into discussions, ask specific people questions, or make sure someone doesn't miss something important. Give it a try next time!

## 🥳 Asiqur ➡️ Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

![cto](https://i.imgur.com/UvT7MUa.png)
<Caption text="S-tier birthday present." />

This one is extra special. 🥹 We're beyond excited to announce that **Asiqur Rahman (Founding Engineer) has been promoted to Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** at Codédex! 

This marks our first ever C-suite promotion, and I couldn't think of anyone more deserving.

Asiqur has been with us for four years, helping build the technology foundation of Codédex. He's grown tremendously as an engineer, manager, and teammate. His recent performance review was nothing short of incredible, with glowing feedback from his peers, direct report, and me.

But what stands out most is that Asiqur **thinks like a founder**. He takes ownership, cares deeply about the product, and constantly thinks about what's best for the company.

As CTO, Asiqur will take on three major responsibilities:

- **Technology Strategy & Engineering Leadership:** Lead our Engineering team, oversee our architecture, and lead the execution of our technical roadmap.
- **Decision-Making & Team Building:** Own Eng hiring and management, make key personnel decisions, and approve technical projects and investments.
- **Budget & Resources:** Manage Eng spending and allocate company resources. Yes, that includes a shiny new company card. 💳✨

It's been an incredible four years, and I'm SO proud of how far he's come. Please join me in congratulating Asiqur on this well-deserved milestone. 👏

Go show him some love on Codédex ([@asiqur](https://www.codedex.io/@asiqur)), IG ([@asiqur.dev](https://www.instagram.com/asiqur.dev)) and LinkedIn.
