### 🧚 Starter Generation with AI

![Builds landing page](https://i.imgur.com/7xsEtBU.png)

You can now generate a starter code and website in HTML, CSS, JavaScript Builds with the help of AI.

You can now either: 

1. Describe your idea via text (the more descriptive the better).
2. Pick from the 3 templates (Personal portfolio, Linktree clone, or Habit tracker).
3. Build from scratch.
