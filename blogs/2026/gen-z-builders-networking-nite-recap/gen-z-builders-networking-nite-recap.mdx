We announced it ten days before and somehow got 700+ RSVPs and **300+ showed up**!

## Fireside Chat with Sonny & Asiqur 🗣️

We kicked off the main programming with a Fireside Chat with me & Asiqur. Ellie facilitated!

We sat down to chat about what it’s like to build a consumer edtech startup from the ground up, challenges of scaling to 2M+ users, and what it takes to land a job or build a company in 2026 and beyond – and to start the nite with some ideas to carry into the convos that followed.

## Guided Networking Session 📛

Next up: networking – but not the awkward stand around and wondering who to talk to kind!

Codédex events like this are all about taking our [community](https://codedex.io/community) offline. We're an online platform, but some of the best moments happen when usernames become faces, Discord friends meet IRL, and ppl who live thousands of miles apart suddenly find themselves in the same room. 

![postcard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codedex-io/blog/refs/heads/main/blogs/2026/gen-z-builders-networking-nite-recap/assets/postcard.gif)
<Caption text="Love these questions! 😍" />

We gave out postcards at check-in with questions on the back, from easy icebreakers to deeper builders questions... and two rounds, too! So you get to meet more people~

This part of the nite was especially fun to watch.

## 📸 Stations

### Tote Bags + Patches

Of course, we couldn't host a Codédex event without giving everyone something to make.

At our DIY tote-making station, attendees could customize their own tote bages with patches and turn them into something completely their own. 

Come for the networking, leave with new friends and a custom tote. Not a bad deal.

### Keycap Fidgets 

Because sometimes the best swag is the thing you can't stop clicking.

Our keycap fidgets gave everyone a tiny piece of keyboard culture to take home – and something to keep their hands busy during the next long coding session.

### LinkedIn Headshots

New connections? Check. New tote? Check. New LinkedIn profile picture? Also check. 📸

Kayla Kwak spent the evening taking professional LinkedIn headshots for attendees!

If you took a headshot, your photo(s) are ready! You can find them in this [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1cAZT_d_f9-upPlHda0Lv_subBDestZ1B?usp=sharing).

## Closing Remarks

At the end of the nite, one thing stood out more than anything else: community. 👯

Codédex may live online, but this community event was a reminder that the relationships built here go far beyond a screen. People came from different places, backgrounds, and corners of tech to meet each other, exchange ideas, and build new connections.

A huge shoutout to [Ellie](https://www.codedex.io/@ellie) and [Gabby](https://www.codedex.io/@gabot) for pulling everything together on an incredibly fast two-week turnaround; rest of the team (Asiqur, Julien, Ally) for helping throughout the nite.
