We announced it ten days before, somehow got 700+ RSVPs, and **300+ showed up**!

## 🗣️ Fireside Chat with Sonny & Asiqur

We kicked off the main programming with a Fireside Chat with me & Asiqur, facilitated by Ellie!

We sat down to chat about what it’s like to build a consumer edtech startup from the ground up, challenges of scaling to 2M+ users, and what it takes to land a job or build a company in 2026 and beyond – and to start the nite with a few ideas to carry into the convos that followed.

## 📛 Guided Networking Session

Next up: networking – but not the awkward stand-around-and-wondering-who-to-talk to kind!

Codédex events like this are all about taking our [community](https://codedex.io/community) offline. We're a global platform, but some of the best moments happen when usernames become faces, Discord friends meet IRL, and ppl who live thousands of miles apart suddenly find themselves in the same room. 

![postcard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codedex-io/blog/refs/heads/main/blogs/2026/gen-z-builders-networking-nite-recap/assets/postcard.gif)
<Caption text="Love these networking questions! 😍" />

At check-in, we handed out postcards with prompts on the back, ranging from easy icebreakers to deeper questions. We did two rounds, giving everyone a chance to meet someone new and make meaningful connections~

This part of the nite was especially fun to watch.

## 📸 Stations

### Tote Bags + Patches

Of course, we couldn't host a Codédex event without giving everyone something to make. ✨

![tote station](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codedex-io/blog/refs/heads/main/blogs/2026/gen-z-builders-networking-nite-recap/assets/tote-station.png)
<Caption text="We even got a heat press to iron on the patches." />

At our DIY tote-making station, attendees customized their own **"i survived my CS semester and all i got was this tote bag"** totes with a bunch of fun patches, turning each one into something completely their own. Some of the patches designed by Gabby: [Grass Touching Club](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcRtXGrEkwd/?img_index=2), [Anti-AI Slop Society](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcRtXGrEkwd/?img_index=3), [LinkedIn Larpers](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcRtXGrEkwd/?img_index=4), [LeetCode Honors Society](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcRtXGrEkwd/?img_index=5), [YC Yearners](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcRtXGrEkwd/?img_index=6), and more!

Come for the networking, leave with new friends and a custom tote. Not a bad deal ;p

### Keycap Fidgets 

Alright. This might be one of my favorite Codédex merch of all-time. I can't stop clicking!

Our customizable keycap fidgets gave everyone something to keep their hands busy during the next project deployment or code generation. We even had three mechanical keycaps to choose from: Codédex Bot, Lumi, and the #30NitesOfCode egg.

## 😋 Food & Drinks 

Last but definitely not least, the food and drinks!

The [Kubo Cafe](https://www.instagram.com/kubocafeny) team absolutely turnt up and made **165 cups of matcha** on the spot. 🍵 There were three flavors: Turon Matcha, Potchi & Cream Matcha, and Buko Mango Cream, all Filipino-inspired and made with love. My biggest regret of the nite is that I didn't get to try one. T.T 

And also shoutout to [Holy Burger](https://www.holyburger.nyc) for keeping everyone fed with sliders throughout the evening! We had Beef Sliders, Chopped Cheese Sliders, Crispy Chicken Sliders (regular + spicy), and Plant-Based Beef Sliders. Nom nom nom fr.

## Closing Remarks

At the end of the nite, one thing stood out more than anything else: community. 👯

Codédex may live online, but this event was a big reminder that the relationships built here go far beyond a screen. It's why we built Codédex in the first place: **for you**. 🫵
