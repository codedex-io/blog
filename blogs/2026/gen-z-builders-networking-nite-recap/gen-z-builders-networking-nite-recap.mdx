## Guided Networking Session

Next up: networking — but not the awkward stand around holding a drink and wondering who to talk to kind.

Our guided networking session gave everyone a reason to meet someone new, start a conversation, and connect with other builders in the room.

For Codédex, events like this are ultimately about taking an online community offline. We're an online platform, but some of the best moments happen when usernames become faces, Discord friends meet IRL, and people who might live thousands of miles apart suddenly find themselves in the same room.

That's what made this part of the nite especially fun to watch.

## DIY Tote-Making Station + Patches & Keycaps

Of course, we couldn't host a Codédex event without giving everyone something to make.

At our DIY tote-making station, attendees could customize their own totes with patches and turn them into something completely their own.

Come for the networking, leave with new friends and a custom tote. Not a bad deal.

## Closing Remarks

At the end of the nite, one thing stood out more than anything else: community.

Codédex may live online, but this event was a reminder that the relationships built here go far beyond a screen. People came from different places, backgrounds, and corners of tech to meet each other, exchange ideas, and build new connections.
