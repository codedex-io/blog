Hi everyone, it's your boy Sonny.

Two years ago, we teamed up with GitHub Education to bring Codédex Club to high school and college students around the world through the iconic [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://www.codedex.io/github-students). 🏕️

Since then, **over 50,000 students** have claimed [Codédex Club](https://codedex.io/pricing) for free, exploring coding, design, and AI, building real-world projects, and leveling up their tech skills along the way.

It's been incredible seeing so many students kick off their coding journeys with us.

Today, we're sharing a small but important update to our Student Developer Pack offer.

---

## What's changing?

Starting **November 1, 2026**, our GitHub Student Developer Pack benefit will change from **6 months to 1 month of free Codédex Club** for eligible students. 

*\*A flower dies somewhere.* 🥀

While the duration is changing, everything you love about Codédex Club is staying the same!

With your free month of [Codédex Club](https://codedex.io/pricing), you'll still unlock:

- 📚 **Full Catalog:** Explore Python, HTML, CSS, JS, SQL, GenAI, ML, UI/UX Design, etc.
- ➕ **Bonus Articles & Cheat Sheets:** Reinforce what you've learned with handy resources.
- 🎴 **Challenge Packs:** Put your coding skills to the test with a challenge on each card.
- 🏁 **Checkpoint & Final Projects:** Submit capstone projects mid-course and at the end.
- 🗓️ **Club Events:** Connect and grow alongside fellow Clubbers.
- 🚀 **Unlimited Builds:** Run scripts and host websites with our online code editor.
- 🤖 **Unlimited Lumi:** Get instant help and tackle roadblocks with your AI companion.

That's one whole month to learn something new and explore everything Codédex has to offer!

**Note:** The only Codédex Club offerings not included are Project Reviews and Course Certificates, as both require the involvement of our human experts.

---

## But but but... why?!

Let's talk numbers. 

Over the past two years, 50,000+ students have redeemed 6 months of Codédex Club through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. 50,543, to be exact!

![github-students](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codedex-io/blog/refs/heads/main/blogs/2026/github-student-developer-pack-update/assets/github-students.png)
<Caption text="As seen from our #github-students channel in Slack." />

That's **over 300,000 months of Codédex Club** given away... for free! At our regular price of \$19.99/month, that's more than **\$6M in membership value** provided to students worldwide. 😮

As a small team, we're incredibly proud of that impact. But authoring new courses, shipping new features, and keeping the lights on all come with real costs. To keep Codédex sustainable for the long run, we've made the difficult decision to reduce the benefit duration to 1 month.

And while this benefit is changing, the need for accessible tech education is only growing.

As our former president said at an Abdul El-Sayed rally just three days ago:

<Quote text="The economy is changing fast and is just going to start changing faster. AI, I know it's been hyped, but there is a real technology that's coming. It is going to change a lot. And we've got to make sure it benefits everybody. We are going to need to revamp our education for the new era that AI is ushering in.” - Barack Obama" />

**Our mission hasn't changed. Not one bit.** 

We still believe learning tech skills should be fun, accessible, and affordable for everyone. ❤️‍🔥

This adjustment allows us to keep introducing students to Codédex Club for free while investing in new learning experiences, better tools, and a stronger community for years to come.

We're so grateful to everyone who's been part of this, and especially to **Morgan Ersery**, **PJ Metz**, and the **GitHub Edu team** for making this evergreen partnership possible. We were even honored with a [GitHub Unverse Award](https://www.instagram.com/p/DG9DYZhuzNZ). We definitely don't take this for granted. 🙏

## Wait, what if I already claimed my free Club?

Don't fret! If you've already claimed your Codédex Club via GitHub Student Developer Pack before November 1, 2026, and are partway through your 6-month membership, you'll still get to enjoy the full duration of your membership. Nothing changes. 👌
