Two years ago, we teamed up with GitHub Education to bring Codédex Club to high school and college students around the world through the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://www.codedex.io/github-students). 💛

Since then, **over 50,000 students** have claimed 6 months of Codédex Club for free, learning to code, design, AI, building projects, and leveling up their tech skills along the way.

It's been incredible seeing so many students start their coding journeys with us!

Today, we're sharing a small but important update to our GitHub Student Developer Pack offer.

## What's changing? 👀

Starting **November 1, 2026**, we're changing our GitHub Student Developer Pack benefit from **6 months to 1 month of free Codédex Club** for eligible students.

While the duration is changing, everything you love about Codédex Club is staying the same!

With your free month of Codédex Club, you'll still unlock:

- 🌈 **Full content catalog:** The Legend of Python, Origins Trilogy, and more
- ➕ **Bonus Articles & Cheat Sheets** to reinforce your learning
- 🎴 **Challenge Packs** to put your coding skills to the test
- 🚀 **Checkpoint & Final Projects** to build something real
- 🗓️ **Community Events** to connect with fellow coders

That's one whole month to learn something new, build a project, and explore everything Codédex has to offer!

## But but... why?!

Let's talk numbers.

Over the past two years, more than 50,000 students have redeemed 6 months of Codédex Club for free through the GitHub Student Developer Pack.

That's **300,000 months of free Codédex Club** we've given away!

At our regular membership price of $20/month, that's approximately **$6M in retail membership value** provided to students around the world.

As a small, independent team, we've loved making Codédex more accessible to students. But building new courses, maintaining our platform, and keeping the lights on all come with real costs.

And while we'd love to keep offering 6 months for free forever, we also need to make sure Codédex is sustainable for the long run.

That's why we're reducing the GitHub Student Developer Pack benefit from 6 months to 1 month.

Our mission hasn't changed: we still want to make learning to code fun, accessible, and affordable. This change helps us continue offering students a free introduction to Codédex Club while supporting the future of our platform.

We're incredibly grateful to everyone who's learned with us through GitHub Education, and we're excited to keep welcoming the next generation of builders. 🚀

Thank you to Morgan Ersery & the GitHub Edu team for helping us.

## Wait! Do I qualify for this?

To qualify for student benefits, you must:

- Have a GitHub account.
- Be currently enrolled in a degree or diploma granting course of study from a recognized educational institution.
- Be able to provide documentation from your school that shows your student status.

## Get started w GitHub Student Developer Pack

If you are already verified via GitHub Education, go to the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) to find the Codédex tile (scroll all the way down).

If you are NOT already verified via GitHub Education, follow these steps:

1. Head over to [Codédex 🤝 GitHub Education](https://www.codedex.io/github-students) page.
2. Click on the [Sign up with GitHub] button.
3. If you don't have your GitHub student account connected to your Codédex account, go to [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) > [Sign up for Student Developer Pack] button.
4. Complete the application with your .edu email and/or upload proof of academic status.
5. Once you’ve uploaded your academic status docs on the following page and submitted them, it will take anywhere from 1 hour to 9 days for GitHub to verify you as a student.

Welcome to the Club! Once you have a Club membership, you should see a rainbow CLUB logo appear in your User Dashboard.
