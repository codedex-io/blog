Two years ago, we teamed up with GitHub Education to bring Codédex Club to high school and college students around the world through the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://www.codedex.io/github-students). 💛

Since then, **over 50,000 students** have claimed [Codédex Club](https://codedex.io/pricing) for free, exploring coding, design, and AI, building projects, and leveling up their tech skills along the way.

It's been incredible seeing so many students kick off their coding journeys with us.

Today, we're sharing a small but important update to our GitHub Student Developer Pack offer.

## What's changing? 👀

Starting **November 1, 2026**, our GitHub Student Developer Pack benefit will change from **6 months to 1 month of free Codédex Club** for eligible students.

While the duration is changing, everything you love about Codédex Club is staying the same!

With your free month of Codédex Club, you'll still unlock:

- 📚 **Full Catalog:** Python, HTML, CSS, JS, SQL, GenAI, ML, UI/UX Design, and much more.
- ➕ **Bonus Articles & Cheat Sheets** to reinforce your learning
- 🎴 **Challenge Packs** to put your coding skills to the test
- 🚀 **Checkpoint & Final Projects** to build something real
- 🗓️ **Community Events** to connect with fellow builders
- 🤖 **Unlimited AI** to supercharge your learning with Lumi

That's one whole month to learn something new and explore everything Codédex has to offer!

## But but but... why?! 🥺

Let's talk numbers. 

Over the past two years, 50,000+ students have redeemed 6 months of Codédex Club through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. 50,543 to be exact as of writing.

![github-students](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codedex-io/blog/refs/heads/main/blogs/2026/github-student-developer-pack-update/assets/github-students.png)
<Caption text="As seen from our #github-students Slack channel." />

That's **over 300,000 months of Codédex Club** we've given away... for free! At our regular price of \$20/month, that's over **\$6M in membership value** provided to students worldwide.

As a small team, we've loved making Codédex more accessible to students. But authoring new courses, shipping new features, maintaining our platform, and keeping the lights on all come with real costs. And we need to make sure Codédex is sustainable for the long run.

That's why we've made the difficult decision to reduce the benefit from 6 months to 1 month.

**Our mission hasn't changed.** We still believe learning tech skills should be fun, accessible, and affordable for everyone, especially in this new era AI usher. This adjustment allows us to continue giving students a free introduction to Codédex Club while investing in new learning experiences, better tools, and a stronger community for years to come.

We're so grateful to everyone who's been part of this journey, and especially to **Morgan Ersery** and the GitHub Education team for helping us make this partnership possible. 🙏
